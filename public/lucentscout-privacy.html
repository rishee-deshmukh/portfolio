LucentScout - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

LucentScout is a personal, single-user software tool created and operated by Rishee Deshmukh ("I"). It helps me find job postings. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not a commercial product.

What the app accesses

With my own explicit consent, LucentScout reads (read-only, using the Gmail scope gmail.readonly) one dedicated Gmail mailbox that receives only job-alert emails from job sites such as Indeed and Jobright. It never sends, deletes, labels or modifies any email.

What it does with that data

  • It extracts job details that the alert emails state: job title, company, location, work setting, salary or tags if shown, and the link to the posting.
  • It stores only those extracted details, plus the email's message identifier and date (so each email is handled once), in a private database that only I control. Email bodies are not stored and not written to logs.
  • To judge how relevant a posting is, the extracted job details (not the email itself) are sent to an AI model service (Google Gemini API) together with my own job preferences.
  • The results are sent to me as private Telegram messages.

Sharing and retention

I do not sell, rent, share or transfer this data to anyone else, and no human other than me reads it. Extracted job details are kept until I delete them. I can revoke the app's Gmail access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and I can delete the stored data at any time.

Google API Services User Data Policy

LucentScout's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
